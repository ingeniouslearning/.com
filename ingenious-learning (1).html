About Us

A learning centre built around each child

Ingenious Learning helps children build strong foundations in reading, writing, maths and creative skills through classes that stay personal, never mass-produced. Parents consistently tell us the same thing: their child started looking forward to class.

Every session — whether it's Jolly Phonics for a four-year-old or Vedic Maths for a teenager preparing for exams — is run in small groups with real, individual feedback, both online and in person at our Chitlapakkam centre.
